## Brand & Style
The design system embodies modern institutional authority, academic prestige, and high-conversion executive clarity for higher technical and executive education. It blends the gravitas of a traditional polytechnic and business institute with the agility and clean precision of enterprise technology platforms.

The target audience encompasses prospective technical students seeking career advancement, working professionals evaluating executive diplomas, and corporate HR leaders looking for enterprise training solutions. The interface must evoke academic rigor, institutional trust, forward momentum, and immediate professional relevance.

The design movement applied is a synthesis of **Corporate Modern** with **Elevated Minimal Architecture**:
- Structural integrity using crisp, clean divisions and light-tinted slate containers.
- Purposeful visual hierarchy directing the eye straight into conversion funnels (syllabus downloads, admissions inquiries, and program enrollment).
- Academic badges, accreditation stamps, and modular curriculum grids balanced by deliberate white space and energetic, performance-driven call-to-actions.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional prestige, digital vibrancy, and conversion-focused visual anchors:

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`)**: Deep Institutional Navy. Serves as the bedrock of structural trust, headers, primary brand marks, and dominant corporate panels.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Blue. Drives primary conversion paths, clickable interactive states, active link states, and primary button hover rings.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Academic Amber/Gold. Reserved strictly for accreditation seals, key performance stats, ranking markers, certification badges, and high-urgency badges (e.g., "Cupos Limitados", "Beca 30%").
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Slate 900. Deployed for high-contrast, ultra-legible typography, footers, and dark-surface executive overlays.

### Surface and Canvas Guidelines
- **Canvas Base**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for core content reading and conversion forms.
- **Secondary Canvas**: Slate Tint (`#F8FAFC`) for alternating program segments, campus tours, and metric bands.
- **Tertiary Container**: Border/Card Slate (`#F1F5F9`) used for input backdrops, subtle dividers, and neutral badge chips.
- **Contrast Rule**: Action buttons must never compete with decorative elements. Amber is for prestige and accents; Royal Blue is the actionable engine.

## Typography
The typography marries the contemporary geometric authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and display units with the neutral, hyper-legible rendering of **Inter** for curricula, course descriptions, data tables, and application forms.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Must maintain tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver an authoritative, polished editorial presence. Program names, degrees, and executive titles should favor 700 or 800 weights.
- **Body & Data**: Inter handles high-density text (study plans, accreditation criteria, academic terms) with generous line-heights (1.5 to 1.6) to guarantee sustained reading comfort.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Used for badges ("Doble Titulación", "Modalidad Híbrida"), form validation, and stats labels, always set in medium or semi-bold with positive letter-spacing for instant legibility at small scale.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate academic catalogs, executive lead-generation funnels, and conversion modules side by side.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (≥1200px)**: 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`, maximum layout container constrained to `1280px` for high readability. Hero and program views use asymmetric splits (e.g., 7 columns for program details, 5 columns for fixed lead capture/application card).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Multi-tier course listings collapse to 2-column cards; admission forms dock below introductory content.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1rem`. Sticky bottom conversion CTAs activate ("Postular Ahora" / "Descargar Malla").

### Rhythm & Alignment
All component margins, gutters, and insets adhere to an 8-point geometric scale (using the specified `space-*` tokens). Section headers use `space-xl` distance from preceding modules and `space-md` separation from their body descriptors.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system avoids ostentatious 3D metaphors, relying instead on **low-contrast outlines** paired with **ambient, tinted depth**.

- **Resting Layer (Cards, Modules)**: Pure white background enclosed by a delicate 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, underpinned by a soft, diffused navy-tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Hover Layer**: When hovering cards or program selectors, the card elevates smoothly with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, shifting the border from `#E2E8F0` to a subtle `#BFDBFE`.
- **Floating Conversion Surfaces (Application Drawers, Sticky CTAs, Sticky Navigation)**: Elevated with `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, backed by a high-clarity background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`.
- **Hierarchy Stacking**: Academic credentials, floating testimonials, and modal dialogs stand on higher z-indices with an active neutral overlay set to `#0F172A` at 60% opacity with 4px backdrop blur.

## Shapes
The design adopts a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness geometry. This reinforces architectural stability, discipline, and modern corporate precision without appearing cold or aggressive.

- **Base Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Input fields, table rows, checkboxes, and inline academic metadata tags.
- **Components (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / 8px)**: Primary & secondary action buttons, program catalog cards, lead form containers, and executive profile images.
- **Floating Overlays & Hero Panels (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / 12px)**: Modal sheets, curriculum syllabus accordions, and major feature banners.
- **Pill Exception**: Numerical rankings, status chips (e.g., "Admisiones Abiertas"), and category badges utilize fully rounded pill contours to distinguish metadata from structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (Direct Conversion)**: Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1E3A8A` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus ring is a clean 3px `#93C5FD` offset by 2px.
- **Secondary Action (Institutional / Inquire)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#1E3A8A`, text `#1E3A8A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Accent High-Priority Action**: Background `#F59E0B`, text `#0F172A` (Semi-bold), reserved for urgent conversion triggers (e.g., "Solicitar Beca").

### Cards (Academic & Executive Programs)
- Structured with a clean 3-part layout:
  1. Top meta strip: Program modality chip ("Online", "Semipresencial") and credential badge.
  2. Core body: Program name in `title-lg`, duration, and starting date.
  3. Action footer: Download brochure icon button paired with an arrow-linked "Ver Plan de Estudios".
- Background is `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2E8F0` and `rounded-lg` (8px).

### Input Fields & Lead Generation Forms
- Background `#F8FAFC`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`.
- Active focus state: Background changes to `#FFFFFF`, border shifts to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).
- Error state: Border `#EF4444` with inline caption typography in red.
- Lead capture blocks integrate floating labels and 1-click WhatsApp/Call enrollment selectors.

### Chips & Academic Badges
- **Accreditation Badge**: Background `#FEF3C7` (light amber), text `#B45309`, 1px solid `#FDE68A`, displaying an official icon (ribbon/seal).
- **Executive Pill**: Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E3A8A`, border `#DBEAFE`.

### Form Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Box dimension 18x18px, border 1.5px `#94A3B8`, radius 4px (checkboxes) and circle (radios).
- Checked state: `#2563EB` fill with crisp white icon checkmark.

### Additional Specialized Components
- **Curriculum Accordion**: Clean slate borders separating academic modules with collapsible term subjects and total ECTS/hours displayed on the right.
- **Stat Metric Callout**: Oversized metric numbers in `display-hero` (`#1E3A8A`) anchored by an Amber underline dot and a secondary two-line description in `body-sm`.
